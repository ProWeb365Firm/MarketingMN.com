Accessibility Statement

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

MarketingMN is committed to providing a website that is accessible and usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

We strive to make https://marketingmn.com/ accessible and easy to navigate by following recognized accessibility practices and considering accessibility throughout the design, development, and maintenance of our Website.

Our Commitment

MarketingMN aims to improve the accessibility and usability of the Website by working toward compatibility with applicable accessibility standards and guidelines, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG).

We consider accessibility across areas such as:

  • Clear and consistent navigation
  • Readable and appropriately structured content
  • Keyboard accessibility
  • Alternative text for relevant images
  • Sufficient color contrast
  • Descriptive links and headings
  • Accessible forms and interactive elements
  • Compatibility with commonly used assistive technologies

Ongoing Improvements

Website accessibility is an ongoing process. We regularly review our Website and may make improvements to its content, design, functionality, and technology to provide a more accessible experience.

Although we strive to make the Website as accessible as reasonably possible, some areas may not yet fully meet every accessibility guideline or may depend on third-party technologies that are outside our direct control.

Need Assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of the MarketingMN Website or encounter content or functionality that you believe is not accessible, we encourage you to let us know through the contact options available on the Website.

When reporting an accessibility issue, please provide as much detail as possible, including:

  • The page or URL where the issue occurred
  • A description of the accessibility problem
  • The device or browser you were using, if relevant
  • Any other information that may help us identify and address the issue

We will review accessibility concerns and consider reasonable improvements where appropriate.

Third-Party Content

The Website may include content, links, tools, or services provided by third parties. MarketingMN does not control the accessibility of third-party websites or platforms and cannot guarantee their accessibility.

We encourage users to contact the relevant third-party provider if they experience accessibility issues with third-party content or services.

Feedback

Accessibility feedback helps us improve the Website. We welcome comments and suggestions that can help make MarketingMN more accessible and easier to use.
