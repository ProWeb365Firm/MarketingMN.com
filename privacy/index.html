Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

MarketingMN (“MarketingMN,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when you visit our website, https://marketingmn.com/ (the “Website”).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you use our Website.

1. Information We Collect

MarketingMN may collect information in the following ways:

Information You Provide

If you contact us through the Website, request a consultation, or otherwise communicate with us, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Company or organization name
  • Information included in your message or inquiry
  • Any other information you choose to provide

We use this information to respond to your inquiries, communicate with you, and provide requested services or information.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Referring website or source
  • Date and time of visits
  • General website usage and interaction information

This information is primarily used for website analytics, performance monitoring, and understanding how visitors use the Website.

2. Google Analytics 4

MarketingMN uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with the Website and to help us improve its content, functionality, and overall user experience.

Google Analytics may collect information such as:

  • Pages viewed
  • Approximate geographic information
  • Device and browser information
  • Traffic sources
  • Website interactions
  • Session information
  • Other usage and technical information

Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect and analyze this information.

The information generated through Google Analytics may be processed by Google in accordance with Google’s privacy practices.

For more information about how Google processes information, please review Google’s privacy information and Google Analytics documentation.

You can also control or limit certain Google Analytics data collection through available browser settings, Google’s tools, or other privacy controls provided by Google.

3. Cookies

The Website may use cookies and similar technologies, including cookies associated with Google Analytics 4.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites recognize returning visitors and understand how websites are used.

MarketingMN uses analytics-related cookies to help us understand Website traffic and usage. These cookies are not used by MarketingMN to intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain Website functionality or the accuracy of analytics information.

4. How We Use Information

MarketingMN may use collected information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and consultation requests
  • Communicate with prospective or existing clients
  • Provide and improve our services
  • Improve Website content, functionality, and user experience
  • Understand Website traffic and visitor behavior
  • Monitor Website performance
  • Identify technical issues
  • Maintain Website security
  • Meet legal or regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

5. How We Share Information

MarketingMN does not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our Website, conduct analytics, communicate with users, or provide business services. These providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to use it only for the purposes for which it was provided.

For example, MarketingMN uses Google Analytics 4 for Website analytics.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of MarketingMN, our users, or others.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including responding to inquiries, maintaining business records, improving our services, complying with legal obligations, and resolving disputes.

The retention periods for analytics information may also be determined by the configuration and retention settings associated with Google Analytics.

7. Data Security

MarketingMN takes reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to or through the Website.

8. Third-Party Websites

The Website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties.

MarketingMN is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may choose not to provide personal information when interacting with the Website. However, certain information may be necessary for us to respond to an inquiry or provide a requested service.

You may also manage cookies through your web browser settings and use available privacy controls provided by Google and other technology providers.

If you have submitted personal information to MarketingMN and would like to ask about that information, you may contact MarketingMN through the contact options provided on the Website.

10. Children’s Privacy

The Website is intended for businesses, organizations, and general audiences and is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

MarketingMN does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Website.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us through the contact options available on the Website.

MarketingMN may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our Website, services, analytics practices, legal requirements, or other business practices.

When we make changes, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how MarketingMN handles information, please contact us through the contact options available on the Website.
